Mushroom Substrate

Against measurements

Each row sets an engine the same task as a published experiment and compares what comes out with what was measured. The green band is the measured range; the dark mark is the engine's result.

Read the two kinds of row differently. Where a value was fitted to the measurement, agreement shows only that the fit worked. Where nothing was tuned, agreement means something, though with this few checks not much. Several measurements come from search summaries of papers that were not opened, and the electrical recordings are themselves disputed. The full account, including what was changed and why, is in docs/CALIBRATION.md.
