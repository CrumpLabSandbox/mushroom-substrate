Mushroom Substrate

Mushroom plugins

Synthesisers, atmospheres, effects, composers and symphonies built on the mushroom models. Each has its own page, its own controls and a drawing of the process that is making the sound. The species are the presets. Every control traces to a named mushroom process or measurement, and each plugin says which of its numbers are assumptions.

physical model a body that vibrates, driven by a force. model signal something the model computes, used as the sound source. model as modulator a model quantity moving an ordinary sound path.

Mushroom WorldEvery plugin here in one: two voices, an atmosphere, an effect and a console, built from one fungus. A proposed single plugin for SynthLand Explorer.

Scale. Every plugin has a Scale switch: the species is grown in the 4K, 40K, 400K or 4M simulator, and the plugin adjusts to what that size has (no flow or cords at 4K, no electrical activity below 400K, years and spores at 4M). Species. 90 real ones and 25 invented ones, which are marked as invented wherever they appear. See every plugin's drawing at once.

Nothing here is a recording of a fungus: mycelium is silent as far as anyone has measured, and real hyphae are too damped to ring. The plan and what each plugin will trace to are in docs/PLUGINS.md.
