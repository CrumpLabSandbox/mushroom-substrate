Mycelium 40K

The field is 25.6 mm across. Brown is wood, olive is leaf litter, red dots are root tips, dark is soil; patches fade as they are used up. Faint lines are hyphae the fungus has abandoned. Discs are fruiting sites, drawn up to button size (a full mushroom would not fit). Lower the temperature to trigger fruiting in species that need a cold spell.

Substrate is counted in units of "the cost of one 0.1 mm piece of hypha". Many values are tuning values, not measurements: see the model notes. About
