Mushroom Substrate

Physical simulators of how fungi grow: mycelial networks, what moves through them, the mushrooms they produce and the substrate around them. One simulator at each of four sizes, and up to 100 kinds of mushroom to load into them.

What is real and what is not

The models follow published work on how hyphae extend, branch, fuse and move resources, and the growth equations were checked against the papers they come from. The numbers are another matter: a small part is measured and most is inferred from relatives or set by hand so the model runs. Most species have no measured value of their own and behave as the default for their network family. Sizes, colours and ranges are general knowledge that nobody checked. Every value carries its basis (sourced with a citation, inferred, or unknown), and the species browser shows it. The model is specified in docs/MODEL.md, and what it rests on is in docs/research.

The data

  • worldbuilder.json: a small pack of every species (form, size, colours, biomes, abundance) for placing mushrooms in another project without running a simulator. What its fields mean.
